# Data Status

This file exists so nobody has to guess what is real and what is a placeholder.

## Included and real

- `data/results/binary_classification_results.csv` — Table 2 of the manuscript,
  extended with 95% CI for AUROC (DeLong method).
- `data/results/multiclass_classification_results.csv` — Table 3 of the
  manuscript, extended with a 95% bootstrap CI for overall accuracy.
- `data/results/baseline_characteristics.csv` — Table 1 of the manuscript,
  independently recomputed from patient-level data (Welch's t-test for
  continuous variables, chi-square/Fisher exact for categorical variables;
  the test used is named in every row).
- `data/results/participant_flow.csv` — the STARD-style participant flow:
  patients screened, excluded by reason, enrolled, and split into training,
  validation, and testing sets.
- `data/results/platform_comparison_test.csv` — an unpaired DeLong
  significance test comparing high-end vs. low-end AUROC for every cardiac
  view. The platform gap is statistically significant for PLAX (P < 0.001)
  but not for A4C (P = 0.439) or A2C (P = 0.864).

Every one of these files was generated by the corresponding script in
[`analysis/`](analysis) and cross-checked against the manuscript before being
committed here. All numbers in this repository are real.

## Not included, and why

- **Patient-level imaging data and video-level AI prediction scores.**
  Withheld to protect participant privacy, consistent with the ethics
  approval referenced in the manuscript. This is also why `data/raw/` is
  empty in this repository — see [`analysis/README.md`](analysis/README.md).
- **LIFES model weights and training pipeline.** Remain the authors' ongoing
  research property and are outside the scope of this results-transparency
  repository.
